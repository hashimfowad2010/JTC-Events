Welcome to JTC Events

Beaconhouse Johar Town Campus • Lahore

Beaconhouse Johar Town Campus (JTC) in Lahore is renowned not just for academic rigor, but for a vibrant student culture that comes alive through its flagship annual events. Among a bustling calendar, two spectacles stand as the pillars of JTC’s co-curricular identity: JTCMUN and Sportsfest. Together, these events capture the dual spirit of the campus—where sharp intellect meets raw athletic drive.

JTCMUN transforms the campus into a bustling arena of global diplomacy and high-stakes debate. For three intense days, students step into the shoes of international delegates, tackling pressing geopolitical crises, drafting complex resolutions, and mastering the art of diplomacy. The corridors buzz with unmoderated caucuses, passionate parliamentary debate, and strategic alliances forged over quick breaks. Beyond committee sessions, JTCMUN features social concert nights and formal dinners building connections across institutions.

In sharp contrast, Sportsfest unleashes pure adrenaline across the JTC grounds. The campus turns into a sea of vibrant house colors as student-athletes gear up for track and field events, high-stakes football matches, cricket showdowns, and fast-paced basketball games. Driven by house rivalries and intense school pride, it is a celebration of grit, sportsmanship, and endurance.

Ultimately, these twin traditions define the essence of student life at JTC. While JTCMUN sharpens the mind and nurtures future leaders, Sportsfest builds character and team spirit—creating a balanced, high-energy environment long after the final committee adjourns and stadium lights fade.
